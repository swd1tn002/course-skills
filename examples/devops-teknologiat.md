## Schedule Gaps and Explanation

### Gap: January 18 - January 18 (Week 3, start of course)

**Status**: First class scheduled on Tuesday, January 19  
**Reason**: Course official start date is Monday, January 18, 2027, but first actual class is on Tuesday, January 19. This allows for one day of preparation.

### Gap: February 23 - March 1 (Week 8)

**Status**: No classes scheduled  
**Reason**: Intensive week period (22. – 26.2.2027, vko 8) + non-teaching period transition. This intensive week falls between Period 3 and comes before the break.

**Academic Calendar Context**:
- Intensiiviviikko: 22. – 26.2.2027 (vko 8), paitsi Vierumäki 1.3. – 5.3.2027 (vko 9)

The course does not schedule regular Tuesday classes during this intensive week period. Students participate in intensive activities instead of regular weekly sessions.

### Gap: March 17 - March 29 (Weeks 12-13)

**Status**: No classes scheduled from March 17 to March 29  
**Reason**: Period 3 ends (19.3.2027), followed by intensive week and transition to Period 4.

**Academic Calendar Context**:
- End of Period 3: 19.3.2027 (vko 11)
- Intensiiviviikko: 22. – 25.3.2027 (vko 12)
- Opetuksettomat päivät: 26.3., 29.3.
- 4. periodi starts: 29.3.2027 (vko 13)

This two-week gap encompasses:
- **19.3.2027**: Period 3 ends (Tuesday, last class of period 3 on 16.3)
- **22-25.3.2027**: Intensive week (vko 12)
- **26.3.2027 (Thursday)**: Non-teaching day (Opetukseton päivä)
- **29.3.2027 (Monday)**: Non-teaching day (Opetukseton päivä) - Period 4 official start
- **30.3.2027 (Tuesday)**: First class of Period 4 resumes

### No Major Gaps: April - May

After March 30, the course has consistent **weekly Tuesday meetings at 11:00-13:45** for the remainder of Period 4. Only minor gaps due to non-teaching days:

- **6.5.2027** (Thursday): Non-teaching day (Opetukseton päivä) - Does not affect Tuesday schedule
- **May Day (1.5.2027)** falls on Sunday, no impact
